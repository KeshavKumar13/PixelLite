Privacy Policy

Information about how PixelLite handles images, analytics and advertising related technologies.

PixelLite is designed to process supported image editing and conversion operations in your browser. Images used with the editor are not normally uploaded to a PixelLite image processing server.

Analytics

PixelLite uses Google Analytics to understand website usage and improve the service. Analytics collection may depend on applicable consent settings.

Advertising

PixelLite may use Google advertising technologies. Advertising and related consent choices are handled through the site's configured consent experience where applicable.
