Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

PixelLite is designed to process images directly in your web browser. This page explains the main privacy practices for the PixelLite website.

Images you edit

When you select an image in PixelLite, the image is processed locally by your browser for editing, resizing, filtering, cropping and compression. PixelLite does not require you to upload the image to a PixelLite server to use the editor.

Analytics

PixelLite uses Google Analytics to understand general website usage and improve the service. Analytics may collect information such as page views, browser or device information and interactions with the website. PixelLite does not send the image files you edit to Google Analytics.

Contact emails

If you contact PixelLite at support@pixellite.in, the information you include in your email is used to respond to your request.

Third party services

PixelLite is hosted using GitHub Pages and its domain and DNS services use third party infrastructure. Their respective privacy and security practices may also apply when you use the website.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when PixelLite's features or services change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Your images stay on your device and are processed locally in your browser.
