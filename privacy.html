Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

1. Overview

PixelLite is a browser-based image editing and conversion service. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use PixelLite and how the website uses analytics, advertising and contact services.

2. Image Processing

PixelLite's image editing and conversion tools are designed to process images locally in your web browser. Images selected for editing, resizing, cropping, compression or conversion are processed on your device and are not uploaded to a PixelLite image-processing server.

3. Information You Provide

PixelLite does not require an account to use its image tools. If you contact PixelLite by email, information included in your message may be received so your request can be answered.

4. Analytics

PixelLite uses Google Analytics to understand website usage, such as pages viewed and interactions.

5. Advertising

PixelLite may display advertising through Google AdSense. Advertising services may use cookies or similar technologies according to applicable consent choices.

6. Consent

Where required, PixelLite uses Google's consent mechanisms to manage consent for analytics and advertising related storage and signals.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

PixelLite's core editing functions do not require cookies. Third-party services such as Google Analytics and Google AdSense may use cookies or similar technologies for their respective functions.

8. Third-Party Services

PixelLite may use third-party services including Google Analytics and Google AdSense. These services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when PixelLite's features, third-party services or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@pixellite.in.

PixelLite processes images locally in your browser. No image is sent to a PixelLite image-processing server.
