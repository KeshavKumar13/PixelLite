Privacy Policy

Overview

We aim to provide a simple, useful and privacy-conscious experience. This policy explains in general terms how information may be handled when you use the website and its tools.

Files and local processing

Whenever a tool supports local processing, files selected for that tool are handled in your browser and are not intentionally sent to us for processing.

Information you provide

You may use the available tools without creating an account. If you contact us, the information you choose to include may be received so that we can review and respond to your request.

Analytics, advertising and similar technologies

The website may use analytics, advertising, cookies, local storage or similar technologies to support functionality, understand usage and display relevant content. Depending on your location and applicable requirements, these technologies may process information such as browser details, device information, approximate location and usage information.

Third-party services

Some content or functionality may be provided by third parties. Such services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We do not control the privacy practices of independent third parties.

Consent and choices

Where required, optional technologies may be controlled through consent choices or your browser settings. You can also adjust relevant browser privacy and storage settings.

Data security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled through the website. However, no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes

This policy may be updated when features, practices or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the contact information provided on the Contact page.

Your files stay on your device and are processed locally in your browser whenever supported.
