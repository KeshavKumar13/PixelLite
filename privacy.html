Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

1. Overview

PixelLite is a browser-based image editing and conversion service. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use PixelLite and how the website uses analytics, advertising and contact services.

2. Image Processing

PixelLite's image editing and conversion tools are designed to process images locally in your web browser. Images selected for editing, resizing, cropping, compression or conversion are processed on your device and are not uploaded to a PixelLite image-processing server.

Local processing: Your image files remain on your device during normal use of the editing and conversion tools.

3. Information You Provide

PixelLite does not require an account to use its image tools. If you contact PixelLite by email, the information you include in your message, such as your email address and message content, may be received so that your request can be answered.

4. Analytics

PixelLite uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website, such as which pages are viewed and how visitors interact with the site. Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies and may collect information such as browser, device, approximate location and website interaction data.

PixelLite's active Google Analytics measurement ID is G-QHZR2VLP4X.

5. Advertising

PixelLite may display advertising through Google AdSense. Advertising services may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure or personalize advertising according to the consent choices available to visitors.

PixelLite's advertising publisher ID is pub-1741589992062370.

6. Consent

Where required, PixelLite uses Google's consent mechanisms to manage consent for analytics and advertising related storage and signals. Visitors may be presented with consent choices and can make decisions about optional analytics and advertising processing.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

PixelLite itself does not need cookies to perform the core image editing functions. Third-party services such as Google Analytics and Google AdSense may use cookies or similar technologies for their respective functions, subject to applicable consent requirements and settings.

8. Third-Party Services

PixelLite may use third-party services including Google Analytics and Google AdSense. These services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. PixelLite does not control how third-party services independently process information.

9. Data Security

Because normal image editing takes place in the browser, PixelLite does not receive the image file as part of the editing operation. You should still use a secure, up-to-date browser and device when handling sensitive files.

10. Children's Privacy

PixelLite is a general-purpose website and is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when PixelLite's features, third-party services or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or PixelLite's privacy practices, contact us at support@pixellite.in.
