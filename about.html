About PixelLite

PixelLite is a simple, browser based image editing tool for everyday image tasks.

What you can do

Use PixelLite to crop, resize, filter and compress images, then download the result in a supported image format.

Privacy focused by design

PixelLite processes the images you select directly in your browser. Your image is not uploaded to a PixelLite image processing server.

Simple and accessible

PixelLite is designed to be straightforward to use on desktop and mobile devices, without requiring an account just to edit an image.

Have feedback?

If you find a problem or have an idea for a useful feature, we'd love to hear from you.
