How to convert JPG to PNG

  1. Open the PixelLite converter.
  2. Upload your JPG image.
  3. Select PNG as the output format.
  4. Download the converted image.

Why convert JPG to PNG?

PNG is useful when you want a lossless image format for further editing or when preserving image quality during repeated editing is important. JPG and PNG have different compression characteristics, so the best format depends on how you plan to use the image.

Private browser based processing

PixelLite is designed to process images locally in your browser. Your image does not need to be uploaded to a PixelLite server for the conversion.
