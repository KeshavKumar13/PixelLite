Crop Image Online

Remove unwanted areas around an image and choose a useful composition directly in your browser with PixelLite.

Open Image Editor

Choose an aspect ratio

PixelLite supports free cropping plus 1:1, 4:3, 16:9 and 2:3 options. Choose the ratio that matches the space where the image will be used.

How cropping works

Upload an image, select an aspect ratio if required, drag across the preview to define the crop area, and apply the crop.

Why crop an image?

Cropping can remove distractions, improve composition, focus on a subject, or make an image fit a required layout.
