When should you resize an image?

Resizing is useful when an image is larger than the space where it will be displayed, when a website requires specific dimensions, or when you want to reduce image dimensions before compression.

Keep the aspect ratio

PixelLite includes an aspect ratio lock. Keep it enabled when you want the image proportions to remain consistent while changing its width or height.

Common uses

Images may need resizing for websites, documents, presentations, email attachments, profile images and other digital content. The appropriate dimensions depend on where the image will be used.
