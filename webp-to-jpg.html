How to convert WEBP to JPG

  1. Open the PixelLite converter.
  2. Upload your WEBP image.

Why convert WEBP to JPG?

Some applications, upload systems and workflows require JPG. Converting a WEBP image can make it easier to use in systems that expect the widely supported JPG format.

Transparency consideration

JPG does not support transparency. If the source WEBP contains transparent areas, those areas may become a solid background in the JPG output.
