PNG versus JPG

PNG is commonly used when lossless image data or transparency is important. JPG is widely used for photographs and can produce smaller files through lossy compression. Converting to JPG is useful when a JPG file is required or when reducing photo file size is a priority.

Important note about transparency

JPG does not support transparent backgrounds. If your PNG contains transparency, the exported JPG may use a solid background instead.
