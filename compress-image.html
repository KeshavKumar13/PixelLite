Why compress an image?

Compression can reduce the amount of storage and bandwidth required for an image. Smaller files can also be more convenient for websites, email and sharing.

JPG and WEBP

JPG and WEBP provide quality controls in PixelLite. Lower quality can produce a smaller file, but excessive compression may reduce visible detail.

PNG

PNG is useful when lossless image storage or transparency is important. A PNG file may not become smaller in the same way as a JPG or WEBP file when changing a quality setting.
