How to compress an image

  1. Open the PixelLite editor.
  2. Upload your image.
  3. Choose the export format and adjust image quality.
  4. Download the compressed image.

Why compress images?

Smaller image files can use less storage and can be easier to share or deliver on websites. The amount of reduction depends on the image, format and selected quality.

JPG, PNG and WEBP compression

Different image formats use different compression methods. JPG and WEBP can provide substantial size reduction when quality is adjusted, while PNG is commonly used when lossless image data is important.

Browser based processing

PixelLite is designed to process supported image operations locally in your browser. Your image does not need to be uploaded to a PixelLite server for normal editing and export.
